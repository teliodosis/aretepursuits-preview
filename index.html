Two kinds of growth

Growing up, and waking up.

Most of us grow in two directions. Growing up is maturing in how we think, relate, and lead. Waking up is recognizing the awareness underneath all of it. I coach both, and each feeds the other.

What leaders tell me changes
  • You stop doing the work and start leading it.

  • You meet hard emotions without being swept away by them.

Why both

Most people arrive through leadership. Many stay for everything else.

teliodosis/ tel·ee·oh·DOH·sis /

A word I coined for the integration of doing and being: purposeful action that flows from presence.

Read “Refining Teliodosis”

The philosopher Ken Wilber describes two kinds of human development. Growing up is the slow maturing of how we think, relate, and lead. Waking up is the direct recognition of the awareness we already are. Each can happen without the other, and each goes further with the other.

Executive coaching is mostly growing up. Sooner or later it also asks who is doing the leading, and that question is where waking up begins. I spent thirty years as a technology executive, and that experience is in every engagement. So is my own waking up: more than a decade of daily meditation and a steady deepening into the awareness this work points toward.

You don’t need to know in advance which kind of coaching you need. Plenty of clients begin with delegation and strategic thinking and end up somewhere they didn’t expect. I wrote about that shift in “Same Coach, Wider Door.”

Tales of Wanderment

Coaching from the road.

In November 2024 I became a digital nomad, traveling the US and coaching over Zoom on the same schedule. Living lightly, a few weeks or months at a time, has become part of my own waking up: each new place loosens another idea of who I am. I write about what the road is teaching me regularly.
